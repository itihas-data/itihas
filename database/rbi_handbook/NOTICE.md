# Reserve Bank of India material

**Source:** Reserve Bank of India, *Handbook of Statistics on Indian States*, editions released on 27 Jun 2016, 24 Jun 2017, 5 May 2018, 3 Mar 2019, 13 Oct 2020, 24 Nov 2021, 19 Nov 2022, 15 Nov 2023, 9 Dec 2024 and 11 Dec 2025. Available from https://rbi.org.in (Annual Publications).

**Copyright:** © Reserve Bank of India. All rights reserved. These files are not covered by the CC BY licence of the rest of ITIHAS.

**Basis for reproduction:** every edition from 2017 to 2025 states on its imprint page: "Reproduction is permitted provided an acknowledgement of the source is made." This file is that acknowledgement. The 2016 edition prints no such statement. Its figures are included on the basis of the statement in every later edition, and they are identical to those in the 2017 edition. Each edition's statement is recorded in `releases_rbi.csv`.

**Removal on request:** if the Reserve Bank of India asks, the RBI material will be removed or replaced with a script that downloads it from the RBI website.

**What was done to the figures:** the values were read from RBI's own spreadsheets, without retyping. Each spreadsheet's SHA-256 fingerprint is in `source_files_rbi.csv`. `value_printed` gives the value as printed (Rs lakh), and `value_rs_crore` gives it divided by 100. Empty cells were dropped. The Reserve Bank of India has not reviewed or endorsed ITIHAS.
