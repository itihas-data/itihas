# Licence notes for the ITIHAS data

ITIHAS combines material under three sets of terms. Each file's terms are listed below.

## 1. `data/`: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

The compilation in `data/` (the selection, dating, coding and structure of the records, the release list, the state codes and the revision triangles) is by Shrihan Jha and Veer Parekh and is licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

You may share and adapt it for any purpose, including commercially, if you credit the source. Please cite the database as shown in `README.md`.

The numbers themselves were published by others:
- **State governments** (Directorates of Economics and Statistics, Finance Departments and Planning Departments), in their own releases, Economic Surveys and budget documents. Each number's source document is listed in `data/releases.csv`. When you use a specific number, please also cite its source document.
- **The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI)**, through its eSankhyiki portal (https://esankhyiki.mospi.gov.in/), releases of 8 Aug 2025 and 17 Apr 2026. Source: Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, Government of India.

## 2. `rbi_handbook/`: Reserve Bank of India

The files in `rbi_handbook/` reproduce figures from the Reserve Bank of India's *Handbook of Statistics on Indian States*. They are **not** covered by the CC BY licence above. Copyright remains with the Reserve Bank of India. See `rbi_handbook/NOTICE.md` for the terms and the acknowledgement.

## 3. Code: MIT licence

The scripts in `scripts/` are licensed under the MIT licence in the repository's `LICENSE` file.
