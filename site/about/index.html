About ITIHAS

ITIHAS stands for Indian Time-series of Initial and Historical Accounts of States; the word also means “history” in Hindi. It is a real-time database of state GDP in India: a record of every published estimate, kept as it was first printed, with its date and source. New official releases replace earlier figures, so earlier versions are easily lost. We built ITIHAS to keep them, to measure how much the figures change, and to study what those changes mean for the fiscal rules that rely on state GDP. To our knowledge it is the first database of its kind for Indian states.
