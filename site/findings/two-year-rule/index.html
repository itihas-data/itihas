The two-year rule

How long does a state’s growth figure take to settle? We compare each estimate with the latest figure for the same year, and group the estimates by their age. Estimates less than two years old agree only loosely with the latest figure. Older estimates agree closely, and the revisions still to come become much smaller at the same point.
